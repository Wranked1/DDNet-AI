One decision every 40 ms

Five steps in DDNet 20 physics, ported from the game's source.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Positions, velocities and hooks of every nearby tee, the freeze tiles and the walls a hook can grab. The world is first rolled forward by the bot's own ping.

  2. 02

    Simulate

    Each plan is simulated 27 ticks ahead, 0.54 s: where to run, when to jump, where to throw the hook, when to swing the hammer.

  3. 03

    Evaluate

    Each plan gets a score: points when the opponent ends up near or in freeze, a penalty when the bot does. Its own freeze weighs one and a half times the opponent's.

  4. 04

    Execute

    The first step of the best plan goes to the server as ordinary client input. Before it is sent, the bot checks that it can still stop or jump clear afterwards.

  5. 05

    Repeat

    Two server ticks later it starts over, with the previous plan as a starting point.

What it does and how to control it

By default it fights anyone nearby except your team, ignored players and anyone who has stood still for over 10 seconds.

Combat

  • Throws the hook from range and holds the grab
  • Drags the opponent to the freeze and throws frozen players into it before they thaw
  • Knows a frozen opponent who can't get out from one who thaws in 3 s, and moves on
  • Doesn't hammer frozen players out of the freeze: a hammer hit unfreezes them
  • Picks targets by threat: whose hook is on it, who hit it, who is closing in, who is reachable

Survival

  • Before every input, checks that a way back from freeze remains
  • Keeps its second jump when freeze is near
  • Does not turn back and forth at the freeze edge
  • Remembers where it froze on each map
  • Compensates for its own ping
  • Stuck in freeze with nobody to help: /kill

Automation

  • With the server field empty, joins the busiest block server without a password
  • Moves on when the server empties
  • With nobody in reach, walks to where others are playing, by a route with no freeze
  • The window restarts the bot if it stops and reports disconnects
  • Updates itself on start and every five minutes

Modes

  • WB on Copy Love Box: holds the wayblock alone, throws arrivals into the freeze and takes it from holders
  • A 1 on 1 duel: turns itself on once the bot accepts a duel; the score shows while it lasts, and every duel with the total is on the Clips tab
  • A second bot (the dummy) on the same server, on the first one's team and on its own CPU core
  • Teammates: never touched; it goes first for whoever goes for a friend and hammers the friend out of the freeze
  • Automatic chat: a line every N seconds, a reply to its nick, replies to words, e.g. "duel" → /accept

Controls

  • Team, war and ignore buttons for every player
  • Pause from any program: Ctrl+Shift+F9
  • F3 / F4 in the window: the bot votes yes / no in a running server vote
  • Starts with Windows, straight to the tray
  • Clips of risky moments, with what the plan expected
  • Commands: !mode, !wb, !duel, !target, !goto and more

Questions

Is it a neural network?

No. It's a search: each plan is simulated in DDNet physics, and the best one is picked by a score with hand-set weights.

Does it press keys on my computer?

No. The bot is a separate DDNet client that joins the server on its own. You can play DDNet on the same PC at the same time.

Does it need a graphics card?

No. The simulation runs on the CPU, and any graphics card is enough for the window.

What language is the app in?

Russian and English. It follows the system language by default; you can change it in the window's settings.

A server with a password?

Enter it on first launch or in the settings. It stays on your computer and is cleared when you move to another server.

It played badly. How do I report it?

Tray icon, “Collect a bug report”. A zip with the clips appears on your desktop. Attach it to a report on GitHub.
